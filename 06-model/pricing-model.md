# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 2 · Revenue Expansion. Value is proven: trials import data (up to ~45%) and modeling usage spikes to ~58%, yet monetization lags: conversion frozen at ~2% (1.87–2.08%, mean 1.99% over 13 months) and only 40% of paid retained at one year, so the structural decisions (where the paywall sits, how revenue scales with collaboration) have not landed.

**Model:** Subscription. Subscription fits continuous value (weekly cash clarity + modeling habit) with retention as the primary lever. A generous freemium instead would starve ARPU and let per-user compute costs scale without revenue; the top-of-funnel job is already handled by the reverse-trial motion.

- **What breaks if freemium:** A free tier generous enough for the solo Aha cannibalizes the 2% who would pay.
- **Why the recommendation below is not that trap:** It gives away only what 13 months of data show already fails to monetize (the solo snapshot and first model, flat at 2% regardless of engagement), so cannibalization risk is capped at the ~2% who pay today while the collaboration paywall prices the moment value is actually felt.
- **Tripwire:** If cycle 2-3 cohorts show current payers downgrading to free without the shared workspace recapturing them, that is the signal to revisit.

---

## Recommendation

Repackage the paywall around collaboration: after the reverse trial, keep the solo cash snapshot (import + auto-categorized view) free; require paid for the multiplayer workspace: invite accountant/bookkeeper, shared models, forecast outputs. Concretely: the first modeling output stays free to create, sharing it or inviting a collaborator requires paid, for the trial owner and their advisor.

**The pricing bet:** Packaging-to-value: validating that the free/paid line sits where users feel they need more (the share-with-accountant moment).

---

## Model signal

Targets the Financial Modeling Feature Usage % leading indicator from M4: solo modeling spikes never moved the 2% ceiling, so the paywall moves to exactly where collaborative value lands; conversions should cluster tightly at the share/invite boundary rather than scatter across solo users who never pay.

---

## Pricing memo (refined, for leadership, under 2 minutes)

FinWise has a monetization problem, not a value problem. Trials import (45%) and model (spikes to 58%), yet conversion sits at 2% and paid retention at 40%: we give the Aha away solo and charge before collaboration lands. Recommendation: keep subscription; move the paywall to multiplayer. Solo cash snapshot stays free post-trial; paid unlocks the shared workspace (accountant invite, shared models, forecast). This places the free/paid line at the moment owners feel they need more, the first model worth sharing, so conversions cluster at the boundary. Downside is capped: free solo gives away only what already converts at 2%, so cannibalization risk is bounded at today's payers while the upside is uncapped. Signal: Financial Modeling % in shared workspaces; success = trial-to-paid above the 2% ceiling within two billing cycles. One-way door: cohort by signup, read over cycles 2–3, guardrail on import. If shared-workspace trials still do not convert, the diagnosis is activation, not packaging, and we say so.
