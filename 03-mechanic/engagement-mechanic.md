# The Mechanic · FinWise

> Module 3 · Retention & Engagement. The engagement mechanic that builds the habit loop, with rationale and a wireframe.

## The mechanic

**Mechanic:** Streak Counter

- **Trigger:** Monday 8am weekly cash summary email + in-app banner: 'Your cash is $X, up/down vs last week. Keep your check-in streak alive.'
- **Action:** Opening FinWise weekly to review auto-categorized cash position plus one headline insight (e.g. subscription total, weeks of runway)
- **Reward:** Fresh cash insight on their own numbers + streak extends (e.g. 5 weeks in a row, 3 weeks in the green) with one new true finding
- **Investment:** One-tap confirm/fix of a category (e.g. confirm Sysco to Food Cost) + keeping the weekly email on, which sharpens next week's auto-categorization and headline

---
## Rationale

The streak is the retention layer under the collaboration bet, not a monetization lever on its own. M1/M4 showed solo engagement spikes (modeling to ~58%) never move the 2% ceiling, so the job here is narrower: keep free users present and checking cash weekly so the M5 invite trigger and the M6 share paywall have an engaged owner to fire at. Session collapse (~14 to ~5 min) and 60% annual paid churn (40% retained at 1 yr) show the weekly habit decays without reinforcement; the streak protects a meaningful run of cash clarity, and each investment (category confirm) sharpens next week's trigger. A user about to churn stays because breaking the run feels like flying blind again. Caveat: if converters disproportionately sent an invoice instead, the retained behavior is different and I would switch to a progress bar toward first invoice paid.

## Pre-work (design inputs)

- **Behavior:** Opening FinWise weekly to review auto-categorized cash position plus one headline insight: the action signaling "I can see my real cash position without bookkeeping"
- **Natural cadence:** Weekly (matches the weekly cash summary email opt-in from Screen 3 and the habit moment: returns weekly to check cash)
- **Loss feel:** Missing this would feel like flying blind again: losing the run of weeks where cash was confirmed under control. If it wouldn't feel like a loss, a streak would be the wrong mechanic.
- **Why Streak Counter over the others:** Progress Bar needs a completion state (no finish line for cash monitoring); Leaderboard needs social comparison (wrong for private cash data among SMB owners); Streak fits a consistent weekly cadence via loss aversion + identity ("I'm someone who stays on top of cash").
